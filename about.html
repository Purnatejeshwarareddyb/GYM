About GYM

GYM is a free, educational exercise reference. The library covers nine categories (Warm-Up, Cardio, Chest, Back, Legs, Biceps, Triceps, Shoulder, Abs), each split into Gym and Home workouts, with primary and secondary muscles, instructions, common mistakes and progressions.

Exercise text is generated from movement-pattern templates and marked draft - needs human review. Demonstration images come from the open free-exercise-db project and are matched by name automatically. GYM is not medical advice or a substitute for a qualified coach.
